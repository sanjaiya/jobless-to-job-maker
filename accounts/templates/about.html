About Jobless to Job Maker

Jobless to Job Maker is a career development platform designed to help job seekers improve their technical skills and prepare for interviews.

Users can practice basic skills, communication, team discussion, technical assessments and interview readiness.

The platform also provides job opportunities where users can explore and apply for suitable jobs.

💼 Find Jobs

Explore job opportunities that match your skills.

📝 Skill Assessment

Test and improve your basic and technical knowledge.

🎯 Interview Preparation

Build confidence and prepare for interviews.
